## Highlights

- Used the NvRTX 5.0/UE5-era base before the 4.27 transition.
- Exposed performance and compatibility regressions that motivated the LTS rebase, including slower matched ray-tracing tests and difficult PhysX integration.
- Remains documented as historical context rather than a supported release line.
