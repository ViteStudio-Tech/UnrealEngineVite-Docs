# ueVite26-AugustMajor

<tldr>
<p>
The August major line advances rendering, platform, plugin and toolchain work through two staging snapshots and the final release branch.
</p>
</tldr>

| Release window | Status | Captured commits | Source branches |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1–26 August 2026 | Latest | 518 | 3 |

## Highlights

- Improves TressFX and SSGI, adds gated bent-normal specular occlusion, and restores several ray-tracing and volumetric-fog paths.
- Updates Intel XeSS to 3.1.0, re-applies XeFG support for UE 4.27, and extends SMAA, D3D12 SBT and resource-transition work.
- Upgrades PhysX Blast and backports Cable Component, RenderDoc, Visual Studio Code, Platform Crypto and Animation Budget Allocator plugins.
- Updates VS 2026/MSVC 14.51 setup and Linux dedicated-server plugin support.
