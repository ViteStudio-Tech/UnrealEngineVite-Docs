ViteX Physics · Physics SDK

Game physics that keeps up with the frame.

ViteX Physics is a custom physics SDK, fully compatible with Unreal Engine 4. It takes the solver advances of PhysX 4 and 5, tunes them for game workloads, and adds GPU-accelerated fluids, cloth, ropes, smoke and fire through NVIDIA Flex and Flow.

Compatibility

A drop-in physics layer for Unreal Engine 4 content.

ViteX keeps Unreal Engine 4's physics API: body instances, physical materials, physics assets, collision profiles, constraints, scene queries, ragdolls and vehicles. Existing gameplay code, Blueprints and content run on it without a physics-specific port.

That is the opposite of the UE5 path, where moving to Chaos means re-validating every physics interaction in a project and accepting a slower solver.

Measured

Faster than Chaos, faster than stock PhysX.

The Box Container Pile benchmark runs 10,005 awake rigid bodies headless, so it measures the solver rather than the renderer. Every SDK uses four solver iterations, a 60 Hz step and 300 measured steps after 30 warm-up steps on the same Intel Core i7-13700K host.

Vite's PhysX build enters the 16.67 ms budget for 60 Hz at three threads. Stock PhysX 3.4 needs six. Chaos never enters it, even at 24 threads.

Capabilities

CPU rigid bodies for gameplay. GPU simulation for effects.

Game-tuned solver

Solver and collision advances from PhysX 4 and 5, selected and tuned for game workloads rather than offline accuracy.

UE4-compatible API

Body instances, physics assets, constraints, vehicles and scene queries behave as Unreal Engine 4 content expects.

Flex on the GPU

Unified particle simulation for fluids, tearable cloth and ropes, with fluid-surface rendering integrated into the 4.27 renderer.

Flow on the GPU

Sparse-grid volumetric simulation for fire and smoke that reacts to the scene.

Destruction

APEX Destruction for existing assets and NVIDIA Blast for large, bond-graph-based fracture.

Fixed timestep

Consistent simulation steps independent of render frame rate, with render interpolation, for stable and reproducible play.

Scale

An instanced subsystem and a native-actor path for thousands of bodies; 1,425 native actors run at 374 FPS in engine.

Console-tuned kernels

Hot loops are FMA-fused and structure-of-arrays, and timed on PS5-class Zen 2 with its single 256-bit FMA pipe.
